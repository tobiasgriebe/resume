## de

::: {.executive-summary}

In meiner Rolle als Engineering Director bei sevDesk GmbH habe ich zwei von drei R&D-Säulen geführt: sechs Engineering Manager disziplinarisch, über 40 Entwicklerinnen und Entwickler indirekt in Product-Feature-Teams und Cloud-Infrastruktur-Teams. OKRs als verbindliches Steuerungssystem eingeführt, DORA-Metriken als gemeinsame Sprache zwischen Führung und Engineering-Teams etabliert, ein unternehmensweites Karriere-Framework mit transparenten Entwicklungspfaden aufgebaut. ## en

::: {.executive-summary}
*PhD computer scientist and engineering leader specialising in building and scaling engineering organisations through managers. As Engineering Director at sevDesk GmbH I led two of three R&D pillars: six Engineering Managers with disciplinary authority, and over 40 developers across product feature teams and cloud infrastructure. I introduced OKRs as a binding steering system, implemented DORA metrics as the shared language between leadership and engineering teams, and established a company-wide career framework with transparent development pathways. Connecting engineering decisions to commercial outcomes and communicating at executive level are consistent threads throughout my work. In my current role at Cloudfactory GmbH I advise enterprise partners on technology and platform strategy, with an active mandate at programme and CIO level at a DAX-listed company.*
:::
