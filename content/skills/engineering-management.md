## de

**Engineering Management & IT-Governance**

Technische Vision und Roadmap-Verantwortung für mehrere Teams und Produkte. IT-Governance: Einführung und Durchsetzung von SAST-Tooling in CI/CD-Pipelines (GitLab, GitHub Actions), obligatorisches Tagging von Workloads, Security Policies. Observability: Datadog, New Relic, PagerDuty, Sentry. On-Call- und Incident-Management-Programme. Architekturdokumentation mit Arc42. DORA-Metriken als Steuerungsinstrument. Organisationsdesign und Karriere-Frameworks für Engineering-Organisationen.

## en

**Engineering Management & IT Governance**

Technical vision and roadmap ownership across multiple teams and products. IT governance: rollout of SAST tooling in CI/CD pipelines (GitLab, GitHub Actions), mandatory workload tagging, security policies. Observability: Datadog, New Relic, PagerDuty, Sentry. On-call and incident management programs. Architecture documentation with Arc42. DORA metrics as a steering instrument. Organizational design and career frameworks for engineering organizations.
