## de

::: {.executive-summary}

## en

::: {.executive-summary}
*PhD computer scientist and engineering leader with proven experience building and scaling engineering organisations in regulated environments. At sevDesk GmbH — a regulated financial software platform serving over 100,000 German SME customers with their accounting, invoicing, and tax compliance — I led two of three R&D pillars as Engineering Director: six Engineering Managers with disciplinary authority, and over 40 developers across product feature teams and cloud infrastructure. The operating model was an equal partnership between an Engineering Director and a Product Director at pillar level — exactly the binary-star model Raisin names. OKRs as a binding steering system, DORA metrics as the shared language between engineering and business, and a company-wide career framework with transparent development pathways are consistent outcomes of my work.*
:::
