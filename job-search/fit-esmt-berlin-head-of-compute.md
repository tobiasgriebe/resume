# Fit Assessment — Head of Compute (m/f/x)
**Company:** ESMT Berlin / DEEP entrepreneurial hub  
**Location:** Berlin-Mitte, Schlossplatz — hybrid, up to 15 days abroad/year  
**Date assessed:** 2026-06-30  
**Source:** jobs.esmt.berlin (id=aff540)

---

## Role Summary

Head of the computing vertical at DEEP, ESMT Berlin's deep-tech company builder. Not a traditional engineering leadership role — this is an ecosystem and programme leadership role: building relationships between startups, researchers, corporates, investors, and policymakers in the semiconductors / cloud / HPC / quantum space. Fundraising and business development are explicitly part of the mandate.

---

## Overall Fit: LOW-MEDIUM (~25%)

The cloud computing knowledge is genuinely relevant and the Berlin location works. But this is fundamentally a startup ecosystem / programme management role, not an engineering leadership role. The domain skews toward semiconductors, quantum, and HPC — areas outside the track record — and the primary deliverable is ecosystem-building and BD, not running an engineering org.

---

## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Role Type — MISMATCH WITH PREFERENCES

This is not a director/VP/HoE role in the sense of leading an internal engineering organisation. It is an external-facing vertical lead at a business school's innovation hub — closer to venture-adjacent programme director than Engineering Director. The preferences explicitly exclude "pure product/PM roles, consulting without leadership scope"; this sits in that grey zone.

The role involves:
- Running programmes (not teams of engineers)
- External relationship management (startups, investors, policymakers)
- Fundraising and BD contribution
- Conference representation

That profile is closer to a startup ecosystem manager or vertical GM than a VP Engineering.

### Technical Domain — PARTIAL MATCH

| Requirement | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 10+ years in semiconductors, cloud, HPC, or quantum | Cloud: yes (AWS/K8s, platform engineering). Semiconductors/quantum/HPC: no background |
| Cloud computing | Strong — core of sevDesk and Würth work |
| Semiconductors / quantum | No — not in background |

The "or" in the requirement technically allows a cloud-only profile, but the vertical is called "Compute" and encompasses semis and quantum prominently. Being cloud-only in a semis/quantum-heavy context is a credibility gap in stakeholder conversations with researchers and hardware specialists.

### Leadership & Stakeholder Skills — PARTIAL MATCH

External relationship management, C-level communication, and cross-functional collaboration are present in the track record. Enterprise consulting (adesso, Cloudfactory) adds credibility in the stakeholder-management dimension. But academic/research institution networks, VC relationships, and policymaker engagement are not demonstrated in the CV.

### Entrepreneurial / Ecosystem Context — WEAK

ESMT DEEP is explicitly a deep-tech company builder. The preferred profile likely has direct startup founding, VC, or accelerator experience. The consulting and corporate engineering background doesn't map strongly here.

### Location — FINE ✓

Berlin-Mitte. No issue.

### Compensation — UNKNOWN ⚠️

Academic/research-adjacent institution roles in Germany frequently pay below the €150k floor. Business schools at this level can pay competitively for senior external-facing roles, but no number is given — must confirm.

### Language — FINE ✓

English fluent (mandatory); German preferred. No issue.

---

## Key Risks

1. **Wrong role type** — ecosystem/programme leadership, not engineering org leadership; misaligns with stated preferences
2. **Semiconductor/quantum gap** — cloud expertise is real but covers only part of the vertical's scope
3. **Compensation** — academic institution context suggests likely below floor
4. **BD/fundraising expectation** — not a natural fit and not a preference

---

## Recommendation

**Do not apply.** The role is interesting intellectually but misaligned in two fundamental ways: it is a programme/ecosystem leadership role rather than an engineering org role, and the domain leans heavily on semiconductors and quantum where there is no background. Compensation risk adds a third strike. The cloud expertise alone is not sufficient to anchor credibility across the full vertical.
