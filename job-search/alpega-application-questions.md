## How does this job align with your career path?

My career has moved in one consistent direction: from leading a single team toward owning the engineering strategy of an entire organisation. I started as a technical team lead in academia and consulting (Universität Duisburg-Essen, adesso), then took on full engineering-org leadership at Würth Cloud Services and sevDesk — architecture, delivery, and people leadership for 40+ developers across multiple teams. In my current role at Cloudfactory, that scope has already widened beyond a single engineering org: I combine engineering delivery leadership with a Product Line Product Management Lead mandate across four platform products at 50Hertz Transmission, working directly with VP/C-level stakeholders on roadmap, architecture, and delivery across a portfolio rather than one team. The VP Software Engineering role at Alpega is the natural next step in that trajectory — taking the modernisation, KPI-driven delivery, and AI-adoption work I've done at Director level and applying it at group scale, with full ownership of the engineering organisation and a direct line to the CEO.
