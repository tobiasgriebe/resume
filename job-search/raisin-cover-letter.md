## Application for Director Engineering – Investment & Pension Domain (m/f/d)

Dear Raisin Hiring Team,

The "binary-star leadership model" you name in your posting is not a phrase I had heard before reading it, but it describes exactly how I have been working. At sevDesk GmbH I served as Engineering Director for two of the three R&D pillars alongside a Product Director counterpart: equal ownership, shared accountability for outcomes, and a clean interface between product strategy and engineering delivery. That model is either natural or it is not, and for me it has been the operating mode for the last three years. Six Engineering Managers with disciplinary authority and indirect responsibility for 40+ developers is the same order of magnitude as the IPP domain.

**The regulated financial software context**

sevDesk is a cloud accounting and invoicing platform for 100,000+ German SMEs, operating under GoBD — Germany's tax compliance regulation for digital accounting systems. That is not investment product engineering, and I will not claim otherwise. What it is: a regulated financial software environment where compliance requirements shape architecture decisions, where data integrity and auditability are non-negotiable, and where the business consequences of engineering failure are immediate and visible to customers. The stack is AWS, Spring Boot/Kotlin, and Kubernetes; the delivery governance used DORA metrics (Deployment Frequency, Lead Time, Change Failure Rate, MTTR) as quarterly steering instruments alongside OKRs. At adesso SE I previously led consulting engagements in the finance and insurance sectors, which gave me an earlier grounding in regulated domain engineering. The investment and pension domain is territory I am approaching without direct product experience. My expectation is that strong technical leads within the IPP teams carry the domain depth, and that the Director's job is to give those leads the structural conditions to perform: clear ownership, delivery discipline, career growth paths, and an engineering voice in product prioritisation. That is the job I have been doing.

**What I would bring to IPP**

The OKR system I introduced at sevDesk connected engineering roadmap to business goals with measurable quarterly targets. The career framework I built created structured development pathways and a performance culture that retained senior engineers through a highly competitive Berlin hiring market. The GitLab-to-GitHub migration I led transformed CI/CD pipelines under a DevSecOps approach, embedding security scanning and compliance checks into every delivery pipeline. These are durable structural improvements, not one-off projects, and they are the kind of work the IPP domain needs to scale.

My salary expectation is 155,000 euros per year. I am available from January 2027 with flexibility on start date and look forward to a first conversation.
