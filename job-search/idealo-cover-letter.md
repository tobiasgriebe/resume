## Application for Director of Engineering – Catalogue (m|w|d)

Dear idealo Hiring Team,

The structure you describe — seven teams, around 50 engineers, led through Operations Leads who own accountability for their teams — is the operating model I have been running for the past three years. As Engineering Director at sevDesk GmbH I led two of three R&D pillars with six Engineering Managers and indirect responsibility for over 40 developers across product feature and cloud infrastructure teams. The job was not to manage the work myself but to make the EMs independently capable of managing it: clear ownership, decision-making authority at the right level, and a performance culture where delivery commitments mean something. DORA metrics (Deployment Frequency, Lead Time, Change Failure Rate, MTTR) were the steering instrument in quarterly reviews, not a reporting exercise; OKRs were the shared language between engineering and product that replaced ad-hoc prioritisation.

**Technical direction without becoming a bottleneck**

Your requirement for "strong technical credibility to challenge and guide senior engineers on architecture without needing to be hands-on in the code" describes a specific operating mode that not everyone who has led engineering teams has actually practiced. At sevDesk I established Architecture Decision Records as the governance instrument for technical choices, set API-first and cloud-native standards for a Spring Boot/Kotlin/PHP stack on Kubernetes/AWS, and co-owned a company-wide application modernisation programme from initiation through delivery. At Würth Cloud Services I held sole architecture ownership for a greenfield cloud platform on Red Hat OpenShift — the full technical design was mine, not delegated. In my current consulting mandate at 50Hertz Transmission GmbH I operate at CIO level, translating platform architecture decisions into programme-level strategy. The technical depth is there; the mode is always to raise the bar for the engineers in the team rather than to replace them.

**Reducing operational load and driving automation**

The Catalogue domain carries the kind of recurring operational complexity — data quality pipelines, merchant offer ingestion at scale, matching and deduplication — where the real leverage is in removing firefighting rather than better firefighting. At sevDesk I led a GitLab-to-GitHub migration that transformed CI/CD pipelines under a DevSecOps approach, embedding security scanning and compliance checks into every delivery pipeline and building the foundation for AI-assisted developer tooling. The instinct to address root causes rather than manage symptoms is not something I had to develop: it has been the consistent approach across every transformation programme I have led.

I do not have a prior background in price comparison platforms or catalogue engineering specifically, and I will not claim familiarity I do not have. What I bring is the engineering leadership infrastructure that allows domain-expert engineers to work at their best. That is the job this role is asking for.

My salary expectation is 145,000 euros per year. I am available from January 2027 with flexibility on start date and look forward to a first conversation.
