# Fit Assessment — Director Engineering, Adtech & Marketplace
**Company:** Redcare Pharmacy — Europe's No. 1 e-pharmacy (SDAX listed)  
**Location:** Berlin (Brückenstraße 5), remote possible  
**Reports to:** Executive Director of Engineering / VP of Engineering  
**Team:** ~40 engineers across 5–7 teams, through 2 Engineering Managers  
**Date assessed:** 2026-09-28  
**Source:** redcare-pharmacy.com, job 744000150083369

---

## Role Summary

Director-level engineering leadership for Redcare's Retail Media and Marketplace domains. Owns engineering direction, EM development, delivery, architecture, technical debt, and commercial outcomes (take rate, margins). Leads through 2 EMs across ~40 engineers in 5–7 teams. Reports to VP/Executive Director of Engineering. No salary stated.

---

## Overall Fit: MEDIUM (~45%)

The organisational structure is an almost exact replica of sevDesk — 40 engineers through 2 Engineering Managers — and the engineering leadership capabilities are a strong match. The gap is domain-specific: the posting explicitly requires experience developing marketplace or adtech platforms. Neither is in the background. The gap is real but less disqualifying than a pure technical specialist role — a Director operates through EMs and can bridge domains faster than individual contributors.

---

## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Engineering Leadership Through Managers — STRONG MATCH ✓

The structural match here is unusually precise:

| Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 3+ years leading multiple teams through managers | sevDesk: Engineering Director Feb 2023–Nov 2025 (2.75 years), 6 EMs, 40+ engineers |
| Developing Engineering Managers | Career frameworks, EM coaching, structured performance management at sevDesk |
| Drive delivery, architecture, technical debt | Application modernisation programme, ADRs, cloud-native migration at sevDesk |
| Build/buy/partner decisions | Make-or-buy decisions across all leadership roles; adesso consulting explicitly covered this |
| Strong English communication | Fully bilingual; applied in international contexts throughout career |

The sevDesk role is functionally identical in structure to what this posting describes: same headcount, same EM-layer, same delivery and architecture ownership scope.

### Domain: Marketplace / Adtech Platform Engineering — GAP ⚠️

The posting states "experience developing commercial B2B platforms like marketplaces or ad tech" as a core requirement. Redcare's context makes this concrete:

- **Marketplace**: third-party seller infrastructure, supply/demand management, pricing engines, listing and search, settlement/payments, take rate economics
- **Retail Media / Adtech**: ad serving, brand advertising products, sponsored listings, bid management, targeting, attribution, viewability measurement

None of this appears in the background. sevDesk is B2B SaaS (accounting software for SMEs) — B2B, but not marketplace or adtech. Würth Cloud Services is a B2B cloud platform for trades — again, no marketplace or advertising dynamics.

This is one of only four stated requirements and is explicitly named, which signals it is a genuine screen rather than boilerplate. At Director level the domain gap is more manageable than at IC level (Directors operate through strong technical leads rather than writing the architecture themselves), but it would be a named challenge in interviews.

### Commercial Acumen — Partial Match

"Connect engineering priorities to commercial outcomes including take rate and margins" is marketplace-specific commercial framing. Tobias has strong evidence of technology-to-business-outcome alignment (OKRs, DORA, Geschäftsführung-level reporting at sevDesk; CIO-level reporting at 50Hertz), but the specific metrics of marketplace economics — take rate, supplier economics, demand-side monetisation — are unfamiliar territory.

### Company & Stage — MATCH ✓

Redcare Pharmacy is SDAX-listed with 4,600+ employees and €1B+ revenue. Established, sustainable, mid-to-large size — fits the preferred company profile. The Berlin office with remote flexibility is ideal. e-pharmacy/health is not an excluded domain.

### Salary — Unknown

Not stated. SDAX-listed company at Director Engineering level in Berlin: likely €130k–€160k range. Needs confirmation against the €150k floor.

---

## Key Risks

1. **Marketplace/adtech domain gap** — explicitly required; one of four stated requirements; would be a named topic in any interview
2. **Salary unconfirmed** — listed company at this level could be at or just below floor; check early
3. **Domain credibility with tech leads** — marketplace and adtech engineers have strong domain vocabulary; Director needs enough fluency to challenge and set direction

---

## Recommendation

**Borderline — low priority until salary is confirmed.** The organisational fit is excellent (identical structure to sevDesk) and the engineering leadership capabilities are well-matched. But the marketplace/adtech domain requirement is genuinely stated and genuinely absent from the background. This is a weaker application than CKM or the confidential CTO role, where the domain match was much closer. If salary clears €150k and the hiring team is open to domain learning at Director level, it is worth a first conversation — otherwise lower priority relative to other open leads.
