Software Development Manager for the Stiftung für Hochschulzulassung — the public foundation that operates Germany's centralised university admissions system. ## Overall Fit: LOW-MEDIUM (~30%)

Tech stack and leadership credentials are a match on paper. But three structural problems make this a weak prospect: almost certain salary miss, scope reduction from manager-of-managers to manager-of-ICs, and a public sector context that scores low on both technical challenge and compensation potential.

## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Role & Scope — PARTIAL MATCH

The role is a single layer of management: 16 individual contributors report directly to this person. Tobias's recent roles operate one level above this — leading Engineering Managers who lead developers. This is a lateral-to-downward step in management depth, not a peer-level role.

No Engineering Manager layer is mentioned. No CTO/VP above the role is described — this appears to be the top of the engineering function reporting directly into foundation leadership.

The architecture strategy and team development responsibilities are genuine and match his profile; the scope ceiling is the issue.

### Compensation — LIKELY MISS ⚠️

"Außertariflich mit Jahressonderzahlung" in a public-sector Stiftung. German public sector IT leadership at this scope typically pays €90–130k. Even the top of that band falls short of the €150k floor. No number given — would need to confirm, but the base assumption is a miss.

### Technical Challenge — WEAK

The tech stack (Java/K8s/Kafka) is solid, but the context is a government-adjacent foundation running a heavily regulated, change-averse national admissions system. Modernisation will be slow and procurement-driven. OZG/SDG compliance work is important civic infrastructure, but it is not the architecture modernisation, AI integration, or platform engineering challenge the preferences prioritise.

### Industry & Mission — NEUTRAL

Public sector / education administration is not an excluded industry. Civic mission (fair university access) is meaningful. But it doesn't score on fintech, SaaS, cloud infrastructure, or any domain where software is a commercial product — the preferences explicitly favour software-as-core-product companies.

### Location — FINE ✓

Berlin office exists. 60% remote is fine. No issue here.
