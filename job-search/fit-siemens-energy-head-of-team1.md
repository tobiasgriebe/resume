**Division:** EVP Global Functions / Digital Products and Solutions

**Salary:** Not stated
**Date assessed:** 2026-10-04

---

## Overall Fit: LOW — do not pursue

The domain adjacency (energy sector, 50Hertz overlap) and Berlin location are real positives. Everything else is a problem. This is a first-line or second-line engineering manager role, not a Director-level position. The level mismatch is severe enough to make this not worth pursuing.

---

## The Core Problem: Level Mismatch

The posting says:

- **Experience band:** "Manager / Team Leader"
- **Leadership requirement:** "2–3 years in team leadership, engineering management, or technical project management"

Tobias has 9+ years of engineering leadership, including 3 years as Engineering Director managing 6 Engineering Managers and 40+ developers. The role asks for someone at the beginning of their management career. In Siemens's job architecture, "Head of Team 1" within a Digital Products unit means first-line manager of one delivery team — not a departmental head with budget and headcount authority.

There is no mention of managing managers, no budget ownership, no headcount authority at scale. The role is structurally 2–3 levels below the current profile.

**Outcome of applying:** Either screened out as overqualified at first pass, or if hired, a significant step backwards in scope, influence, and almost certainly compensation.

---

## Domain: Energy Sector — Partial Credit

The 50Hertz mandate at Cloudfactory is the closest thing — Tobias is operating at programme level inside a DAX-listed energy transmission company. Energy Management Applications (grid management software, energy balancing, SCADA-adjacent digital products) is not the same domain, but the energy infrastructure context is at least familiar framing. This would not be the issue; the level is.

---

## Siemens Energy as an Employer — Structural Concern

Even if the level were right, Siemens Energy at ~100,000 employees represents a substantially different operating context: large-corporate governance, matrix structures, longer decision cycles, and typically a more constrained scope for engineering leadership than a scale-up or mid-size SaaS company. The "energy transition" mission is compelling, but the operational environment is the opposite of the autonomy-oriented roles where Tobias has been most effective.

---

## Recommendation

**Pass.** The level mismatch alone disqualifies this. Applying would either result in immediate screening-out or, if successful, a career step backwards. No tailored materials warranted.
