## Application for VP Engineering (f/m/d)

Dear Green Fusion Team,

35 people, five squads, a company in growth mode — and the task of turning that growth into a scalable engineering organisation. That is exactly the challenge I took on as Engineering Director at sevDesk GmbH: six Engineering Managers, 40+ developers, two of three R&D pillars, delivery processes, career frameworks and technical standards built from scratch. The title was Engineering Director; the scope and responsibility were those of a VP Engineering. That is the context I bring to your role.

**Structural clarity as a leadership task**

Five squads need shared standards, not shared bureaucracy. At sevDesk I built a company-wide career framework and performance management system that made development paths transparent and gave Engineering Managers the tools to own team quality and delivery independently. DORA metrics served not as a control instrument but as a shared language between leadership, teams and the product organisation. Discovery and delivery boundaries were defined explicitly — the result was a measurable reduction in scope creep and materially more stable planning.

**Technology modernisation as an organisational task**

Technical debt is not a technical problem — it is a decision problem. At Würth Cloud Services I migrated an entire platform from a grown legacy architecture to OpenShift/Kubernetes in an environment that had to combine DAX-group IT governance with start-up pace. At sevDesk I initiated and led an application modernisation programme on Kubernetes/Docker in AWS, running in parallel with a full migration of the development infrastructure from GitLab to GitHub under a rigorous DevSecOps approach. That migration was not just an infrastructure change: it created the technical foundation for targeted AI-assisted developer tooling — something I am actively working on today as Head of Consulting at Cloudfactory GmbH.

**The energy transition as motivating context**

In my current role I advise 50Hertz Transmission GmbH on building a scalable digital developer platform — energy infrastructure is not an abstract sector for me, but a context where engineering excellence has direct societal impact. Green Fusion takes that context further by integrating hardware, algorithms and software into a coherent product system. That combination is more interesting to me than another SaaS stack.

My salary expectation for this role is €148,000 per year. I am available at short notice and look forward to a first conversation.

Best regards,

Dr. Tobias Griebe
