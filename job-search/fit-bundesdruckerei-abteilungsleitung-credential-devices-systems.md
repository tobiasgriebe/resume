### Domain: Secure Software Development, Digital Identities, Credentials — FUNDAMENTAL GAP ⚠️

This is the defining requirement for the role and the decisive disqualifier. "Credential Devices & Systems" at Bundesdruckerei means:

- **ePassport / eID / nPA systems** — government-grade identity document technology
- **PKI and certificate lifecycle management** — issuing, revoking, and managing X.509 certificates at scale
- **Smart card technology** — chip programming, cryptographic protocol implementation (PACE, EAC, BAC)
- **HSM (Hardware Security Module) integration** — tamper-resistant cryptographic hardware
- **FIDO2 / WebAuthn** — modern passwordless identity standards
- **BSI-regulated secure software development** — Common Criteria, IT-Grundschutz, ITSEC evaluation

None of this appears in the background. Tobias's "security" experience is DevSecOps (security scanning in CI/CD pipelines, dependency auditing, software supply chain security at 50Hertz) — enterprise tooling-layer security, not identity systems engineering. The Supply Chain Security Platform at 50Hertz is about build pipeline integrity, not digital credentials or PKI. These are different disciplines with different technical vocabularies and different certification regimes.

This is not a gap that can be framed away. A department lead for Credential Devices & Systems will be expected to make architecture decisions about cryptographic protocols, evaluate vendor HSMs, and engage with BSI certification processes. Without that foundation, the role cannot be credibly held.

### Company Context — Friction

Bundesdruckerei GmbH is 100% state-owned (Bundesministerium der Finanzen). Implications:

- **Compensation**: Federal/quasi-public pay structures. Tech leadership roles at this level likely fall in the €90k–€130k range — well below the €150k floor. No salary stated.
- **Security clearance**: Almost certain requirement (Sicherheitsüberprüfung) for a department lead in credential production. Standard process but relevant to timeline.
- **Culture**: Heavily regulated, compliance-first, slow decision cycles. Very different operating tempo from SaaS/consulting/platform engineering environments.
- **Mission**: Critical national infrastructure — important work, but the engineering challenges are domain-specific rather than architectural or organisational.
