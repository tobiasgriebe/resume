## Key Risks

1. **Title screen** — if they run hard on "prior CTO or VP Engineering," the Engineering Director title gets filtered before a human reads the substance
2. **Hardware context** — their product is hardware + software; no hardware background could be a cultural mismatch for product discussions
3. **Salary** — 100-person climate tech is a wide range; not guaranteed to hit floor
4. **Scale** — 35 people under VP is modest; the preference is for orgs with room to grow, which this has, but starting headcount is lean

## CV Tailoring Proposals (if pursuing)

1. **Reframe scope explicitly** — subtitle "VP Engineering · Engineering Organisation · Platform Engineering"; opening summary must make the VP-equivalent scope unmistakable
2. **Lead with org-building track record** — career framework, EM hiring, team structure from scratch; these directly answer "35 people across 5 squads need clarity"
3. **Tech debt / modernisation as centrepiece** — both major roles have legacy-to-cloud as core mandate; this maps exactly to their stated platform scalability concern
4. **AI angle** — practical AI adoption (not theory): GitHub Actions AI tooling foundation + Cloudfactory developer platform with AI tooling strategy
5. **Climate mission** — brief, genuine mention in cover letter that the energy transition domain is a motivating factor; they're 100 people in climate tech, mission matters to them
6. **Downweight hardware references** — do not claim hardware experience you don't have; keep framing software/platform/cloud
7. **Profile base:** `engineering-director-de.yaml` with subtitle adjusted; summary rewritten to VP-level scope framing
