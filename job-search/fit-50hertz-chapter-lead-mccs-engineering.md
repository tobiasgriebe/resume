## Key Risks and Open Questions

1. **Conflict of interest with Cloudfactory contract** — this is the most immediate practical question. Tobias is employed by Cloudfactory and embedded at 50Hertz under a consulting agreement. Applying for an internal 50Hertz role could create contractual friction (non-compete, notice periods, client conflict clauses). This needs to be understood before any application or conversation is initiated. The right path may be a quiet informal conversation with the hiring manager first, given the existing internal relationships.

2. **Salary unknown** — 50Hertz / Elia Group tech leadership compensation is generally solid for a regulated infrastructure company, but it typically follows a different structure than VC-backed tech (collective agreements, Tarifvertrag, or executive-track banding). Needs to be confirmed against the €150k floor before investing time.

3. **Sourcing/offshore model depth** — the role repeats offshore sourcing management as a requirement. The CV has vendor management context but not explicit multi-geography sourcing at scale. Worth preparing a concrete narrative here.

4. **Chapter Lead scope** — the MCCS cluster is one product cluster within 50Hertz's broader technology organisation. The engineering chapter could range from 10 to 40 people depending on the cluster size. If it is on the smaller end, this may represent a scope reduction from sevDesk's 40+ engineers across two pillars. The permanent nature and strategic energy infrastructure context are genuine compensating factors.

5. **MCCS domain** — MCCS (likely Market Coupling / Capacity Scheduling or similar) is a specific, regulated energy market systems domain. The Chapter Lead role is primarily organizational rather than technical-domain-specific, but some energy market systems context would help in stakeholder credibility within that cluster.

## Recommendation

**Strong candidate — pursue, but navigate the Cloudfactory transition carefully.** The capability match is high across every stated requirement, and the insider knowledge of 50Hertz makes this a qualitatively different opportunity from an open-market application. The permanent role in critical energy infrastructure is also a strong career stabiliser after a consulting engagement.

Practical first step: have an informal conversation with a known 50Hertz stakeholder (someone already in the hiring orbit through the current mandate) to understand the role scope, salary band, and whether an internal-transfer framing is appropriate — before submitting a formal application that goes through Cloudfactory's client relationship.

If salary clears €150k and the Cloudfactory transition can be managed cleanly: **apply with high confidence.**
