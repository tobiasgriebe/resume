## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Technical Stack — STRONG MATCH ✓

| Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Java or Kotlin | Spring Boot/Kotlin at sevDesk; Java at adesso. Kotlin is primary at sevDesk — exact match. |
| AWS | sevDesk ran on Kubernetes/Docker on AWS — primary cloud platform |

| Distributed systems design | Cloud-native architecture ownership at Würth (Red Hat OpenShift/K8s), modernisation programme at sevDesk, ADRs and API-first standards |

The Kotlin + AWS combination is rare enough to be a genuine differentiator. Most candidates at this level will have Java or AWS; fewer will have both alongside a production Kotlin track record.

### Scale-Up Leadership Through Growth — STRONG MATCH ✓

15 engineers growing to 30 in 18 months is precisely the inflection point where informal engineering leadership breaks down and formal management structures need to be built. This is the transition Tobias has navigated from both sides: he built a team from zero at Würth Cloud Services, and he inherited and restructured a 40+ person organisation at sevDesk — including building the career framework, EM coaching infrastructure, and performance management system that allowed the org to scale. Building from 15 to 30 with quality is the core of what the sevDesk Engineering Director role demonstrated.

### Regulated Financial Software — PARTIAL MATCH (sufficient)

The posting asks for "payments or another regulated environment." Tobias has regulated financial software (sevDesk: GoBD-compliant cloud accounting for 100,000+ German SMEs) — not payments processing. The distinction is real: payment rails, PCI-DSS compliance, transaction processing, settlement, and reconciliation are specialist domains not in the background. However, the "or another regulated environment" clause is deliberate and honest, and sevDesk's GoBD compliance (digital accounting regulation from the German tax authority) is a genuine regulated environment requiring audit-readiness and compliance-aligned infrastructure.

The SMB focus is a direct match: sevDesk serves the same customer segment as this company. The commercial and technical instincts developed in that context transfer.

### Hands-On Leadership — MATCH WITH NUANCE

The posting calls this "a hands-on leadership role" with "strong hands-on background." At 15 engineers the CTO will inevitably be more involved in technical decisions, architecture reviews, and code-adjacent work than at sevDesk (40+ engineers). Tobias's Kotlin/AWS/K8s background is current and deep enough to credibly own architecture calls and engage at a technical level with senior engineers. The framing is "hands-on background" — past tense — not "writes production code daily." Architecture ownership at Würth (sole design responsibility) and ADR governance at sevDesk support this. The 50Hertz current role adds platform strategy depth.

### Hiring, Development and Retention — STRONG MATCH ✓

Full hiring ownership at sevDesk (including hiring a peer Engineering Director), built the Würth team from scratch, established company-wide career frameworks and performance management at sevDesk. Retention through a competitive Berlin tech hiring market during 2023–2025 is demonstrated.

### Commercial Partnership — STRONG MATCH ✓

A seat on the leadership team with the CEO and founders, translating commercial goals into a technical roadmap — exactly the operating mode at sevDesk (OKRs as the shared language between commercial objectives and engineering delivery, Geschäftsführung-level reporting). The 50Hertz mandate adds CIO-level stakeholder management and programme-level strategy ownership.

### Salary & Equity — BEST IN CURRENT PIPELINE

€160K–€190K base + 0.5%–1% equity at a 6-year profitable company processing €2B/year. The equity is real value at a profitable company (not a pre-PMF bet), and the base exceeds the target floor comfortably. This is the strongest compensation package seen in the current search.

## Key Risks

1. **Company unknown** — recruiter search with company withheld. Due diligence on the company, founders, and culture is only possible after the intro call. Payments fintech that is profitable for 6 years and processes €2B/year for European SMBs is a narrow field — a short list of candidates will emerge in the intro call.
2. **Payments domain gap** — not a hard requirement ("or another regulated environment"), but will be a topic in a technical deep dive. Need to be prepared to discuss what transfers and what does not.
3. **"Hands-on" expectations** — need to confirm in the intro call what "hands-on" means to this company at CTO level. At 15 engineers it could mean active code review, architecture spike ownership, or genuine IC contribution. The answer changes the job significantly.
4. **Team size (15 engineers)** — meaningful step down in org scope from sevDesk (40+). Upside is the CTO scope and equity; downside is the current team is smaller than what Tobias has been managing.

---

## Recommendation

**Pursue — high priority.** Stack match, SMB context, regulated environment, org-building experience, and compensation package all align. Start with the intro call to identify the company and calibrate the "hands-on" expectation. If both pass, this is one of the strongest applications in the current pipeline.
