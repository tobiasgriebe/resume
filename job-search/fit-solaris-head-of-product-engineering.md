# Fit Assessment — Head of Product & Engineering (m/f/d)
**Company:** Solaris (formerly Solarisbank) — Banking-as-a-Service platform  
**Location:** Berlin — hybrid  
**Reports to:** Executive Director of Technology  
**Scope:** Foundational capabilities: core APIs, developer tools, internal automation  
**Salary:** €120,000 – €140,000 gross (stated in posting)  
**Date assessed:** 2026-08-26  
**Source:** greenhouse.io/solarisbank job 8689553002

---

## Overall Fit: MEDIUM (~45%) — DO NOT APPLY

The role has genuine technical substance and the combined P&E leadership pattern is familiar. However, two clean blockers make this a pass: the salary ceiling is definitively below the stated floor, and the scope is narrower than the experience level warrants. The banking regulation context and IAM/OAuth technical depth requirements add further friction without upside.

---

## Blocker 1: Salary — HARD BLOCK

Stated range: **€120,000 – €140,000**. Minimum target: **€150,000**. The ceiling of this band is €10k below the floor. This is not a gap to negotiate around — the band is published. Pass on this basis alone.

---

## Blocker 2: Role Scope — STEP SIDEWAYS

The title is "Head of Product & Engineering" but the mandate is narrower than it sounds. The role covers one domain within a larger technology organisation: foundational platform capabilities (Core APIs, developer tools, internal automation). It reports to an Executive Director of Technology, meaning there is a significant layer above. This is a department head role, not an Engineering Director / VP Engineering role. Tobias's sevDesk scope (two of three R&D pillars, 40+ engineers, direct influence on company-wide architecture and headcount budget) is broader. Taking this role would be a lateral move at best in terms of organisational scope.

---

## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Combined P&E Ownership — MATCH

The pattern of owning both product roadmap and engineering execution is the same as at CKM and sevDesk. The 50Hertz Product Line PM Lead mandate (4 technical platform products including an Internal Developer Portal) directly maps to the "internal platforms and technical API products" framing in the posting.

### Developer Portal / Backstage — PARTIAL MATCH

The posting explicitly names Backstage as the developer portal stack in use. The 50Hertz mandate includes product ownership of an Internal Developer Portal as one of the four platform products — this is product-level ownership rather than hands-on Backstage implementation, but it is the correct frame of reference. Not a gap, but not a perfect signal either.

### Technical Depth: IAM, OAuth, OIDC, API Gateway — GAP

The posting requires "strong understanding of IAM, authentication flows (OAuth, OIDC), API gateways/proxies." This is the most specific technical requirement in the posting and is not directly evidenced in Tobias's background. sevDesk and Würth Cloud Services involved API-first architectures and Kubernetes/Docker, but not authentication platform engineering at depth. For a role that explicitly owns Auth services, Policy engines, and IAM as core product areas, this is a real technical gap.

### Regulated Environment — PARTIAL MATCH WITH SIGNIFICANT DELTA

The posting requires experience scaling platforms in regulated environments. sevDesk (GoBD-compliant cloud accounting) and Würth (DAX-group IT governance) both qualify. However, Solaris is a fully licensed German bank under BaFin supervision. Banking regulation is categorically heavier than GoBD: capital requirements, payment services regulation, audit trails, data residency, and compliance overhead are materially different. The gap is bridgeable for a seasoned leader but requires disclosure.

### Distributed Systems, Microservices, SRE — MATCH

Kubernetes/Docker on AWS (sevDesk), Red Hat OpenShift (Wurth), Datadog/New Relic/PagerDuty observability, on-call programmes, incident management — the platform engineering fundamentals are well covered.

### AI Integration — MATCH

Explicitly mentioned as a requirement ("AI tools, thinking critically about AI outputs"). This is covered in both the Cloudfactory/50Hertz context and the sevDesk GitHub Actions/DevSecOps platform work.

---

## Key Risks

1. **Salary ceiling** — €140k max is €10k below stated floor; no ambiguity
2. **Scope reduction** — department head within a larger tech org, not an org-wide Engineering Director
3. **IAM/OAuth technical gap** — architectural expertise required in a domain not prominently in the background
4. **BaFin regulation** — steep learning curve; would need to be positioned as a strength rather than a gap
5. **Solaris context** — company is "transitioning to AI-native banking platform," which often signals significant internal restructuring; check for signs of instability

---

## Recommendation

**Pass.** Salary is a hard block — the published ceiling does not reach the minimum. Even setting salary aside, the role scope is narrower than current positioning warrants, and the IAM/Auth technical depth requirement adds a non-trivial gap without compensating upside. The technical domain (developer platform, internal APIs) is interesting but available at other companies with better compensation.
