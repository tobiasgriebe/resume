## Overall Fit: LOW-MEDIUM (~32%) — Pass

The strategic and organisational leadership dimensions map well — OKRs, roadmap, architecture strategy, process improvement are all demonstrated strengths. But the role is Head of R&D at a hardware product company whose core output is precision audio electronics and automotive integration. No background in hardware product development, analog/digital electronics, DSP, or automotive OEM engineering creates a domain gap that is difficult to bridge at this leadership level. Compensation at a boutique audio manufacturer is also unlikely to clear the floor.

### Domain: Hardware Product R&D — FUNDAMENTAL GAP ⚠️

Burmester's R&D output is physical audio hardware: reference amplifiers, preamplifiers, DACs, streaming devices — and increasingly automotive-integrated audio systems for Mercedes-Benz and Porsche. The Head of R&D is expected to own architecture and platform decisions across this product range. That requires grounding in:

- **Analog and digital electronics** — circuit design, component selection, signal integrity
- **Digital signal processing (DSP)** — audio processing chains, filter design, latency management
- **Embedded systems** — firmware, RTOS, hardware abstraction layers
- **Automotive OEM integration** — CAN/LIN/MOST/Ethernet protocols, AUTOSAR, OEM homologation processes for Mercedes/Porsche
- **Hardware product lifecycle** — NPI, EVT/DVT/PVT, CE/FCC/EMC certification, manufacturing ramp

None of this is in the background. Tobias's engineering leadership is entirely in cloud software (SaaS, Kubernetes, microservices, CI/CD). The "complex systems development" requirement is interpreted very differently here: the complexity is hardware-software co-design and automotive integration, not distributed cloud infrastructure.

This is a more manageable gap than Bundesdruckerei's identity/credential specialisation — because the role is leadership-weighted and the posting lists "IT" as an acceptable degree alongside "electrical engineering." Burmester does have a growing software component (streaming platform, DSP software, app integration). But a Head of R&D who has never developed a physical product would face credibility challenges in every architecture discussion, every hardware design review, and every OEM negotiation with Mercedes or Porsche.

### Company Context — Friction on Multiple Dimensions

**Size**: Burmester is a boutique manufacturer — likely 50–150 employees. This falls below the preferred company size range (100–2,000+) and means the R&D team is probably 10–30 people. The scope is narrower than sevDesk's 40+ engineering org.

**Compensation**: Owner-led, niche-premium audio company. Revenue is significant (OEM contracts with Mercedes/Porsche) but not at SaaS scale. Head of R&D compensation at a company of this profile typically runs €100k–€140k — below the €150k floor. No figure stated; "attraktive Vergütung" is boilerplate.

**Product market**: High-end audio is a specialist, slow-moving market. The engineering challenges are interesting but the business context (ultra-premium hardware, OEM B2B, long product cycles) is far from the growth-stage software environments Tobias has operated in.

**Competition**: 104 applicants is unusually high for a specialised R&D leadership role, suggesting significant general-market interest. Candidates with both audio/electronics domain knowledge and leadership experience will rank ahead in a technical interview.

## Recommendation

**Pass.** The strategic and process leadership capabilities genuinely match what the posting describes — OKR introduction, platform strategy, roadmap management, org development. But the Head of R&D role at a hardware company requires enough domain credibility to lead hardware architects and defend decisions to automotive OEM partners. Without electronics or embedded systems background, that credibility is hard to establish. The compensation profile adds a second independent reason to pass.
