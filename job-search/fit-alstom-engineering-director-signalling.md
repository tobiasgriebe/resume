---

## Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

### Role Scope — SURFACE MATCH ONLY

150–170 direct/indirect reports is a larger org than any prior role. The management responsibilities (resource planning, workload monitoring, stakeholder negotiations, tender support) are generically familiar. But the entire substance of the role is railway signalling project engineering — this is not a software engineering leadership role, it is a rail systems engineering leadership role.

### Technical Domain — HARD DISQUALIFIER ✗

| Requirement | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Signalling technology experience | No — zero background |
| ETCS onboard systems (preferred) | No |
| Engineering degree in electrical engineering or railway technology | PhD is computer science — adjacent but not the field |
| Cost/planning control in technical (rail) environments | General project management yes; rail-specific no |

Alstom's signalling engineering teams work on safety-critical, homologated railway systems. A director without domain knowledge cannot credibly lead technical negotiations with rail operators, oversee safety system delivery, or represent engineering in tender examinations. This is not a gap that strong general leadership compensates for.

### Industry — MISMATCH

Rail infrastructure / mobility hardware is outside all prior experience. Not an excluded industry (no ethical concern), but also not in the open-to list. The role sits at the intersection of hardware, safety certification, and public infrastructure procurement — a very different world from B2B SaaS or cloud platforms.

### Location — FINE ✓

Berlin office. No issue.

### Compensation — UNKNOWN

DAX-adjacent large industrials typically pay well for director-level roles (likely €130–160k range), but the role is not a fit regardless.

### Language — FINE ✓

Fluent German and English. No issue.

## Recommendation

**Do not apply.** The domain gap (railway signalling / ETCS) is absolute — it is the core technical substance of the role, not a nice-to-have. No CV tailoring bridges the absence of signalling engineering expertise. Alstom will screen for domain knowledge at the first technical interview at the latest.
