## CV Tailoring Proposals

### 1. Center the narrative on "Engineering Management at Scale"

The JD is almost entirely about managing managers, setting engineering culture, and driving measurable outcomes — not hands-on technical work. Your CV currently leads with technology specifics (K8s, AWS, CI/CD) that are relevant to an Engineering Director building product systems, but noise for this role. Reframe both sevDesk and Würth to put org design, team lead development, and KPI-driven delivery at the front of each role description.

**Concrete change:** In the sevDesk entry, move the "Führung & Organisationsentwicklung" section above the technical sections. For this application, that is the most important part.

### 2. Quantify leadership scope explicitly up front

DKB specifies "~90 staff, 5 direct reports" as context-setting numbers. Match that language. Lead each role with your numbers:

> *"Disziplinarische Führung von 6 Engineering Managern, indirekte Verantwortung für 40+ Engineers"*

Put this in the role headline or a first-line summary — don't bury it in paragraph three.

### 3. Translate OKR / KPI steering into the JD's language

The JD specifically mentions "Engineering KPIs für Steuerung, Priorisierung und kontinuierliche Verbesserung." Your CV describes OKR frameworks and measurable quarterly goals — these are the same thing, just different vocabulary. Add an explicit sentence:

> *"Einführung von Engineering-KPIs zur messbaren Steuerung von Delivery-Performance und kontinuierlicher Verbesserung innerhalb der R&D-Organisation."*

### 4. Surface the AI-in-engineering angle

DKB explicitly wants someone who "understands how AI transforms engineering workflows." Your GitLab→GitHub migration included "gezielte Integration KI-gestützter Entwicklungstools und automatisierter Code-Quality-Pipelines." That sentence already exists in your CV — but it's buried in the middle of a paragraph. Pull it out as a named initiative or highlight.

### 5. Bridge into the banking/regulatory context via Würth and sevDesk

You have two regulatory angles that are weak but real:
- **sevDesk:** GoBD-compliant cloud infrastructure — this is financial/accounting regulation, closely adjacent to the Finance processes DKB cares about
- **Würth:** Architecture decisions aligned with corporate IT governance and security standards; "konzernkonforme" change management processes

Neither is BaFin, but together they show you've operated in compliance-conscious, audit-relevant environments. Use this framing in the cover letter to close the gap.

### 6. Add a line on "Chapter Lead / Spotify model" awareness in the cover letter

DKB's use of "Chapter Lead" signals a Spotify-style org model (tribes, squads, chapters). Your CV doesn't mention this explicitly, but your experience building the Platform Engineering pillar as a standalone unit is essentially chapter/guild thinking. Name it: show you understand the model and have operated this way.

### 7. What to de-emphasize for this role

- Technical stack details (Kotlin, PHP, React, Docker, Kubernetes) — cut from the headline bullets; mention briefly in tech context only
- CI/CD pipeline specifics — signals "DevOps engineer" more than "engineering management leader"
- Project-level delivery details (scanandpay, mobile apps, etc.) — not relevant; this is about managing the engineering org, not individual deliveries

### 8. Profile and language recommendation

**Use:** German-language profile (`engineering-director-de.yaml` base, with the framing adjustments above)  
**Subtitle to adjust:** Consider "Führung Engineering-Organisationen · KPI-gesteuertes Delivery-Management" instead of the current subtitle to mirror DKB's language
