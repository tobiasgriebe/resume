## Company Stage & Size

**Preferred stage:** Growth or late stage (Series E+), or established/profitable companies — proven product, sustainable business, enough organizational maturity to have real engineering infrastructure.

**Preferred size:** Mid to large (roughly 100–2000+ employees). Enough scale for a genuine engineering org; not so small that the role collapses into hands-on IC work.

**Ownership model:** No preference — VC-backed, bootstrapped, corporate subsidiary, or public company all work if the role and mandate are right.

**Engineering org quality:** The ideal org already has a strong engineering culture in place, experienced senior engineers worth working with, and a clear product direction — while still having room to shape, improve, and leave a mark. Not looking to fix a completely broken org from zero, but also not just maintaining the status quo.
