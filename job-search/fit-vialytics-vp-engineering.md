# Fit Assessment — VP of Engineering (m/f/d)
**Company:** vialytics — AI-powered road management platform  
**Location:** Berlin or Stuttgart, remote-first  
**Reports to:** Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO)  
**Scope:** Engineering, Platform, Data Science  
**Stage:** Series A ($10M), ~100 employees, 900+ municipalities, 7 countries (incl. US)  
**Date assessed:** 2026-09-18  
**Source:** career.vialytics.com, job 7673008

---

## Role Summary

Most senior engineering leader at vialytics — owns Engineering, Platform, and Data Science, reporting to the CPTO. Primary mandate: scale the engineering org from ~30 to 70+ engineers, build the leadership layer (EMs), establish delivery rhythms and architecture standards, and lead AI-native engineering adoption. Core product is a computer vision / AI platform for road damage detection used by 900+ municipalities across 7 countries including the US.

---

## Overall Fit: MEDIUM-HIGH (~62%)

Strong match on the engineering leadership, B2B SaaS, product-minded framing, delivery infrastructure, and AI-in-engineering dimensions. The main gap is Data Science / ML ownership — vialytics's core product is computer vision, and the VP of Engineering co-owns the Data Science function. No DS/ML background means limited technical credibility in that half of the role. Stuttgart presence is a secondary practical concern. Salary unknown.

The B2B SaaS engineering leadership match is probably the best in the entire job search so far. sevDesk is directly analogous — growth-phase SaaS, engineering org build, OKRs, platform modernisation.

### Product-Minded Leadership — STRONG MATCH ✓

The posting explicitly wants a "product-minded engineering leader." The 50Hertz Product Line PM Lead mandate — simultaneously running product strategy and engineering for a technical product line — is direct evidence. The posting's emphasis on "product trios from discovery through delivery" and "outcome-driven approach" maps precisely onto how sevDesk and the 50Hertz mandate operated.

### AI-Native Engineering — MATCH ✓

"Enthusiasm for AI transformation in engineering with clear perspective on value-add and adoption strategies" — this is the Cloudfactory/50Hertz work: AI-enabled developer tooling as a platform strategy element, not vision. The sevDesk GitHub/DevSecOps migration as the foundation for AI-assisted code quality is concrete evidence of the "adoption strategies" framing.

### Data Science / ML / Computer Vision — GAP ⚠️

This is the primary gap. vialytics's core product is computer vision (road damage detection from images processed by mobile devices or vehicles). The VP of Engineering co-owns the Data Science function and is expected to:

- "Connect AI research to product and engineering execution"
- "Scale computer vision and advanced AI capabilities including LLMs"
- Partner with or lead data scientists working on CV model pipelines, training infrastructure, and model deployment

No DS/ML or computer vision background is in the profile. The gap is real but not absolute — many strong engineering leaders manage DS teams effectively through excellent hiring of a Head of Data Science and strong cross-functional alignment. The posting's framing ("connect AI research to product execution") is partly organisational, not purely technical. But in a company where the core IP is a CV model, the VP of Engineering will face technical credibility questions in architectural discussions about model training, inference latency, dataset management, and MLOps.

This gap should be addressed directly in any application: the strategy would be to hire a strong Head of Data Science as a first priority, and position the value-add as the engineering rigour, delivery frameworks, and platform infrastructure that DS teams often lack.

### Location: Stuttgart Presence — Practical Concern

The posting lists Berlin or Stuttgart as home bases, but asks for "regular onsite presence in Stuttgart." From Berlin, Stuttgart is a 4.5-hour train journey or 90-minute flight. Regular presence (likely weekly or bi-weekly) is a material travel commitment. Worth clarifying frequency in a first conversation.

### Company Stage & Scale — MATCH ✓

Series A, ~100 employees, 7 countries including US — exactly the preferred stage (growth-phase, international, proven product, sustainable direction). The 30-to-70 engineer scaling mandate is a genuine org-build challenge rather than BAU management. B2B/govtech (municipalities) is not an excluded domain.

### Compensation — UNKNOWN

Not stated. Series A German company at this scale: likely €130k–€160k range depending on equity structure. Needs confirmation before investing in materials. May be at or just below the floor with equity upside.
