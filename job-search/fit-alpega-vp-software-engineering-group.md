# Fit Assessment — Vice President Software Engineering (Group)
**Company:** Alpega Group
**Location:** Barcelona (ideally), with flexibility across Amsterdam / Berlin
**Team:** Group-wide engineering, across the full product portfolio
**Date assessed:** 2026-07-20
**Source:** job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/alpega/jobs/4919596101

---

## Role Summary

Group-level VP Software Engineering at Alpega, a pan-European logistics/TMS (Transportation Management System) software platform (~450–500 employees, ~$105M revenue, Gartner Magic Quadrant Challenger 2026) serving shippers and carriers across 80 countries. Reports directly to the CEO, owns engineering across the whole product portfolio. Mandate: modernize architecture toward cloud-native/API-first, establish engineering KPIs and lift deployment frequency, build a high-performing "builder, not caretaker" engineering culture, and embed AI-assisted development. No salary stated; candidates asked to state expectations during application.

---

## Overall Fit: MEDIUM-HIGH (~60%)

Strong match on mandate — modernization, KPI-driven delivery, AI-assisted engineering, CEO-level stakeholder management, "builder mindset" — is close to a direct description of the sevDesk and Würth work. The primary gate is scale: the posting explicitly wants someone who has "led large (100+), distributed SaaS engineering organisations," and your largest direct org (sevDesk, 40+ engineers via 6 EMs) is well below that bar. The Berlin-flexible location and logistics domain (on your open-industries list) remove two risks that show up in other assessments.

| Requirement | Evidence |

This is close to a checklist match on the operational side of the role.

### Organisational Scale — GAP ⚠️ (primary gate)

The JD asks for someone who has "led large (100+), distributed SaaS engineering organisations." Your largest direct leadership scope is sevDesk: 6 Engineering Managers, 40+ engineers. That's a real and significant jump — larger than the DKB 40→90 gap assessed previously, and this time framed as a hard "have done this before" requirement rather than a target scope to grow into.

Mitigating factor: the current Cloudfactory engagement combines Head of Consulting (engineering delivery) with Product Line Product Management Lead across 4 platform products at 50Hertz — evidence of operating at a broader, multi-team, portfolio level even if headcount doesn't reach 100 directly. Worth leading with in a cover letter, but it's a narrative bridge, not a clean match.

### Domain: Logistics / Freight Tech — NO BLOCKER ✓

Logistics is explicitly on your "open to" industries list. No prior TMS/freight-specific experience, but the JD doesn't ask for domain expertise — it's an engineering leadership and platform modernization mandate. Alpega's B2B SaaS platform model (shippers/carriers/logistics providers) is structurally similar to sevDesk's SME SaaS context.

### Company Stage & Size — GOOD FIT ✓

~450–500 employees, ~$105M revenue, established/profitable-leaning logistics software group, recognized in Gartner MQ. Sits comfortably in your preferred "growth or established, mid-to-large" band — not a pre-revenue startup, not a giant conglomerate.

### Location — GOOD FIT, MINOR FLAG

Barcelona is the stated preference, but Amsterdam and Berlin are named as flexible alternatives. Should confirm early how strict the Barcelona preference actually is for a Group VP role reporting to the CEO — some C-suite-adjacent roles nominally allow remote/flexible but expect regular HQ presence. Not a deal-breaker given explicit Berlin flexibility in the posting.

### Compensation — UNKNOWN, LOWER RISK THAN USUAL

No number stated; you're asked to provide expectations during application. Given the role reports directly to the CEO and owns the full product portfolio's engineering org, the ceiling should be well above your €150k floor for a company at this revenue scale — but confirm before investing heavily, and be prepared to state your €150k+ expectation directly in the application.

## Key Risks

1. **Scale gap (100+ engineers)** — your largest direct org is under half that; this is the single biggest thing an interviewer will probe.
2. **Barcelona pull** — flexibility is stated, but a CEO-direct VP role may informally expect more HQ presence than the posting implies.
3. **Compensation unconfirmed** — no range given; must state expectations proactively rather than wait to be asked.
4. **Group-wide, multi-product scope** — broader than any single-product mandate you've held; the 50Hertz Product Line PM Lead (4 platform products) is the closest evidence but is a part-time/dual mandate, not a standalone precedent.

---

## Strengths to Lead With

1. **Modernization + KPI-driven delivery track record** — DORA/cycle-time work at sevDesk is a near-literal match to "improve quality, boost deployment frequency."
2. **AI-assisted engineering, named explicitly** — concrete and recent (GitHub Actions AI tooling, sevDesk).
3. **CEO/C-level stakeholder pattern** — Cloudfactory's 50Hertz engagement and sevDesk's Geschäftsführung reporting line both evidence operating at this altitude.
4. **Portfolio-level scope precedent** — Product Line PM Lead across 4 platform products at 50Hertz, held concurrently with engineering delivery leadership, is the strongest available answer to "have you operated across a whole portfolio, not just one team."
5. **B2B SaaS domain familiarity** — sevDesk's SME SaaS model transfers directly to Alpega's shipper/carrier platform context.

---

## Recommendation

**Worth applying, with the scale gap addressed head-on rather than glossed over.** The operational mandate — modernization, KPI-driven delivery, AI adoption, CEO-level stakeholder management — is a strong match. The 100+ engineer bar is the real risk; frame it honestly as "largest direct org is 40+, but current mandate spans a 4-product portfolio at C-level" rather than stretching the sevDesk numbers. Confirm the Barcelona expectation and state your €150k+ comp floor proactively in the application.
