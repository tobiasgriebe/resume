# Fit Assessment — Director, Global Enterprise Applications
**Company:** tonies GmbH  
**Location:** Düsseldorf / Berlin / London (hybrid, 30% in-office)  
**Source:** https://tonies.jobs.personio.de/job/2480455  
**Assessed:** 2026-06-01

---

## Role in One Sentence

Lead a company-wide migration from Microsoft Business Central to SAP S/4HANA Cloud while building and running tonies' global Enterprise Applications function (ERP, CRM, e-commerce integration) — reporting to the CIO.

---

## Overall Fit: LOW (≈ 30%)

This is not a software product engineering role. It is an enterprise IT director / business systems role. The core mandate — ERP transformation, SAP S/4HANA implementation, Order-to-Cash / Procure-to-Pay / Record-to-Report governance — sits in a domain that is largely absent from your CV. The job description explicitly names this as a poor fit for candidates whose background is in infrastructure or DevOps rather than "business technology," which is closer to your world than the role requires.

**Recommendation: Do not prioritize this application.** It is a significant stretch with a very low conversion probability at the screening stage. Only worth pursuing if you are actively interested in pivoting toward enterprise IT / ERP leadership as a career direction.

## If You Apply Anyway — CV Tailoring

If you decide to pursue this (e.g., tonies as a brand appeals, or as a long shot), here is how to maximize your chances:

### 1. Lead with the Würth angle — it is your best proxy for enterprise IT

Würth Cloud Services sits inside the Würth Group — a €20B+ global trade group with full ERP/SAP infrastructure. Reframe the Würth role to emphasize:
- Architecture decisions made *in alignment with Würth Group IT governance and security standards*
- *Structured change management processes* for integration into corporate IT infrastructure
- *Regular reporting at corporate/board level*
- Managing within a large enterprise's IT governance framework

This is the closest your CV gets to enterprise IT leadership. Make it the centrepiece, not a supporting bullet.

### 2. Reframe the sevDesk modernization as a business systems transformation

The sevDesk application modernization program had business process implications (GoBD compliance, accounting workflows). Emphasize:
- Ownership of systems that directly supported Finance and core business operations
- GoBD-compliant cloud infrastructure — signals awareness of regulatory/business-process requirements
- Migration program affecting cross-functional business stakeholders, not just engineering teams

Avoid framing it as a Kubernetes/DevOps story (which it primarily is). Frame it as a business-aligned platform transformation.

### 3. Address the ERP gap directly in the cover letter

Do not ignore it. A strong application would acknowledge the SAP gap and position your transformation/migration leadership as directly transferable, with an explicit commitment to learning the SAP-specific domain fast. A vague application that says nothing about ERP will be screened out immediately.

### 4. Profile to use: `default-en.yaml` with targeted edits

- Move Würth to the top of experience or ensure it is prominently featured
- Add a summary line positioning you as a "technology transformation leader with enterprise IT governance experience" rather than "Engineering Director"
- Trim the technology-heavy bullets (K8s, GitHub Actions, Datadog) — these signal the wrong track for this role
- Expand on stakeholder, vendor, and budget ownership language

### 5. What NOT to emphasize for this role

- Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, CI/CD pipelines — this signals DevOps, which the JD explicitly says is a poor fit
- AI integration, developer tooling, code quality pipelines
- Career framework / engineering performance management (it's a minor plus, not a differentiator here)
- Product feature development ownership

## Summary

tonies is an interesting company (public, consumer product, international, Berlin office available) and the role is at the right seniority level. But the domain fit is poor: this is an ERP transformation and business applications leadership role, and your CV reads as a product engineering director. The SAP/ERP gap is very likely disqualifying at the screening stage. This role would represent a significant career pivot into enterprise IT — worth pursuing only if that is an intentional direction, not as a standard application in your current search.
