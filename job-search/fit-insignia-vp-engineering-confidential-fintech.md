## Recommendation

**Worth a scoping conversation before a full application.** The operational mandate is a strong match, but this is one of the few assessments in your pipeline where the risk runs toward "too small/undefined" rather than "too large a scale-jump." Before writing tailored materials, ask Insignia Search directly for: (1) company name and funding stage, (2) current in-house engineering headcount and 12-month hiring plan, (3) a compensation range. If there's a credible team-building runway and comp clears €150k, this is a strong narrative fit worth pursuing.
